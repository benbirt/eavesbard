# E11 — Gemma 4 instead of Whisper for speech-to-text

**Status:** first round done (2026-10-10). Not adopted: Whisper transcribes better at the same speed or faster.

## Setup

- **Clips:** 60 lines from the held-out scene set (`src/eval/test-set.ts`), each spoken by one of ten macOS voices (`say`): British, American, Australian, Irish, South African and Indian English, some natural-sounding and some synthetic. Converted to 16 kHz mono. Their text is known, so word error rate (WER) can be measured exactly.
- **Music:** Tabletop Audio's "Tavern Music" mixed underneath, at 10 dB below the speech and at the same level (0 dB).
- **Models**, on an Apple M5 GPU in headless Chrome through the app's own code:
  - Whisper tiny, base and small, loaded as the app loads them (full-precision encoder, 4-bit decoder);
  - Gemma 4 E2B with its audio encoder (q4f16), using the model card's speech-recognition instruction.
- **Scoring:** WER after lower-casing, removing punctuation and writing small numbers as digits.
- Run with `experiments/e11/stt-eval.mjs`; results in `experiments/e11/results/run1.json`.

## Results

| Model | Clean | Music 10 dB below | Music as loud | Time per clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whisper tiny | 17% | 27% | 103% | 0.16 s |
| Whisper base (app default) | 11% | 18% | 92% | 0.23 s |
| Whisper small | **7%** | **13%** | **33%** | 0.50 s |
| Gemma 4 E2B | 21% | 34% | 55% | 0.51 s |

Over 100% means a model wrote more wrong words than were spoken: tiny and base invent text when the music is as loud as the speech.

By voice (clean), Gemma is weakest on synthetic-sounding voices (Fred 57%, Flo 39%, Eddy 29%) but still behind Whisper base on the natural-sounding ones (about 10% against 7%; Whisper small about 3%).

## Findings

- Gemma 4 E2B transcribes worse than Whisper base and much worse than Whisper small, and isn't faster: it takes as long as Whisper small. Replacing Whisper would save a download of 120–590 MB but cost accuracy, so it isn't worth it.
- **Whisper small** is the most accurate in every condition, by a wide margin with loud music, at 0.5 s per clip (a sixth of real time) on the M5. It's a candidate for the default on capable machines (download about 590 MB against 205 MB for base); phones need checking.
- Caveats: text-to-speech isn't real table audio (crosstalk, distance from the mic, several people). Real recordings would make this more trustworthy.
- Not tried: Gemma judging the scene straight from audio, which would lose the transcript the timeline relies on and, given its transcription quality, is unlikely to beat Whisper plus Gemma.
