Design notes

Who: a GM mid-session, glancing at the screen from across the table. It must be trusted at a glance and corrected in one tap; everything else can wait.

  • The scene is the hero. Art, scene name and music lead; model calls, countdowns and costs move to the log.
  • Nothing over the art. The licence forbids covering notices and signatures, so the art sits whole in a frame; a blurred copy becomes the page's ambient background, and controls live on glass cards over the blur.
  • The intensity colours the chrome (green, amber, red), never the art.
  • One-tap corrections: pause, a different track, volume and output on the main view. Proposed: tap a setting or intensity to set the scene yourself.
  • Rare things in one place: Setup holds models, listening, output, the manual track picker and About (credits and the Fan Content notice).
  • Before a session the side column becomes a “Set the scene” card with a readiness checklist (downloads), instead of loading bars scattered around.
  • Health sits in the top bar as chips (hearing speech, scene model ready); problems turn a chip amber and say what to do.

Typography: Cinzel for scene and track names, the system font for everything else. Dark only, for a dim room.
